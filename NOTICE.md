metadata and notices. This handoff does not change that project's licensing.

The exact files listed in docs/migration/source-manifest-20261026.json are curated
source from the owner's DPA-2026 repository, published under the owner's approved
2026-10-26 handoff scope. Original content is tracked by source commit/blob/SHA-256.

or third-party binary dependencies are imported.

No separate project-wide source license was present at the selected DPA-2026
baseline. This transfer grants no new license and does not relicense those files
under the desktop package's MIT metadata. Preserve this notice when extracting
or modifying them; resolve an explicit license before redistribution as a package.
